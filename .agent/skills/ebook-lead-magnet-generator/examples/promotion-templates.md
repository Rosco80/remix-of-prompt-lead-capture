# Promotion Templates

## Landing Page Structure

```markdown
## Landing Page Copy

### Headline

[Primary benefit or transformation - max 10 words]

### Subheadline

[Expand on headline, mention the format - max 20 words]

### Hero Image

[Mockup of ebook/lead magnet]

### Problem Statement

[1-2 paragraphs describing the pain point this solves]

### What You'll Learn

✅ [Benefit/outcome 1]
✅ [Benefit/outcome 2]
✅ [Benefit/outcome 3]
✅ [Benefit/outcome 4]
✅ [Benefit/outcome 5]

### What's Inside

**[Page count] pages covering:**

- Chapter 1: [Chapter title + brief description]
- Chapter 2: [Chapter title + brief description]
- [Continue for key chapters]

### Who This Is For

This [ebook/guide] is perfect for you if:

- [Qualifier 1 - situation or goal]
- [Qualifier 2 - situation or goal]
- [Qualifier 3 - situation or goal]

### About the Author

[Photo + 2-3 sentence credibility statement]

### Form

**Get Your Free Copy**
[First Name field]
[Email field]
[CTA Button: "Download Now" / "Get Instant Access"]

### Trust Elements

- "Join [X] others who've downloaded this guide"
- [Company logos if applicable]
- "No spam. Unsubscribe anytime."

### Footer

[Privacy policy link] | [Terms link]
```

## Email Sequence

### Email 1: Delivery (Immediate)

```markdown
**Subject:** Your [Lead Magnet Name] is ready 📥

**Body:**
Hey [First Name],

Thanks for downloading [Lead Magnet Name]!

Here's your copy: [Download Link/Button]

**Quick tip:** Start with Chapter [X] if you want to [quick win].

I'd love to know what brought you here—just hit reply and let me know.

Talk soon,
[Name]

P.S. Keep an eye on your inbox. Over the next few days, I'll share
some bonus tips to help you [achieve outcome].
```

### Email 2: Value Add (Day 2)

```markdown
**Subject:** The #1 mistake with [Topic]

**Body:**
Hey [First Name],

How's it going with [Lead Magnet Name]?

I wanted to share something that trips up most people when they
first try [topic]:

[Mistake description + why it happens]

Here's what to do instead:
[Solution in 2-3 bullet points]

This connects to Chapter [X] in the guide—give it another look
if you haven't already.

More tips coming soon!
[Name]
```

### Email 3: Case Study/Proof (Day 4)

```markdown
**Subject:** How [Person/Company] [achieved result]

**Body:**
Hey [First Name],

Quick story for you:

[2-3 paragraph case study or success story related to ebook topic]

The key takeaway? [One-sentence lesson]

If you're ready to get similar results, [soft CTA or next step].

[Name]
```

### Email 4: Conversion (Day 6-7)

```markdown
**Subject:** Ready for the next step?

**Body:**
Hey [First Name],

By now you've had a chance to go through [Lead Magnet Name].

If you've been implementing what you learned, you're probably
starting to [early result].

But here's the thing—the guide only covers the fundamentals.

If you want to [bigger outcome], you might be interested in
[Product/Service]:

[2-3 bullet points on what it includes]

[CTA Button: Learn More / Book a Call / Start Trial]

Questions? Just reply to this email.

[Name]
```

## Subject Line Formulas

| Formula                        | Example                                  |
| ------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------- |
| Your [Resource] is ready       | "Your SEO Checklist is ready 📥"         |
| The #1 mistake with [Topic]    | "The #1 mistake with landing pages"      |
| How [Person] [achieved result] | "How Sarah doubled her leads in 30 days" |
| Ready for the next step?       | "Ready for the next step?"               |
| Quick question about [Topic]   | "Quick question about your marketing"    |
| [Number] tips from [Resource]  | "3 tips from your new ebook"             |
